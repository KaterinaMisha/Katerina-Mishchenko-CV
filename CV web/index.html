Vintage
in Aarhus

ABOUT

Vintage in Aarhus is a website showcasing three vintage and second-hand shops in Aarhus, Denmark. I aimed to capture the unique atmosphere of each store through my own photography and videos, using different colour palettes to reflect their individual character and identity.

INSPO

My inspiration came from Sex and the City, known for its iconic fashion and bold, creative outfits. I was inspired by the characters' unique styles and the way they mixed different pieces to create looks that are now considered vintage. I wanted to bring that same playful and nostalgic feeling into my website.

Soy Milk
Packaging Design

ABOUT

I wanted to create my own soy milk brand because I noticed that there weren't many soy-based milk options on supermarket shelves. I believe soy milk is a great plant-based alternative, with nutritional benefits and a positive impact on the environment. My goal was to create a product that feels approachable, fun and appealing through playful illustrations and thoughtful packaging design.

INSPO

The visual direction was inspired by Japanese milk packaging, known for its playful illustrations, unique typography and charming details. I wanted to bring that same fun and creative feeling into my design, making the packaging more eye-catching and memorable while keeping a cosy, modern aesthetic.
